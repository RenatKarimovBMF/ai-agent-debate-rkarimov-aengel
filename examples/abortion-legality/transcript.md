The law conscripts parental care all the time; what it forbids is abandoning a vulnerable life you brought into being. 'Consent to drive isn't consent to donate blood' fails too: if your driving injures someone, you owe restitution and duties you didn't 'consent' to. Causation creates obligation.

On unsafe abortion: my opponent invokes the WHO, but that same WHO fact sheet concedes that abortion safety depends overwhelmingly on legal context AND medical resources—meaning the humane response is expanding maternal healthcare and post-abortion care, not declaring the killing of the unborn a right. The 'safe versus deadly' frame quietly ignores that every safe abortion still ends a distinct, living human organism with its own DNA and beating cardiac activity.

Extending: a just society measures itself by protection of the most defenseless. The unborn child cannot speak, vote, or flee. 'My body, my choice' cannot answer for the second body—the one with a separate heartbeat and a separate future—that the procedure deliberately ends. Autonomy is precious, but it ends where another human life begins.
