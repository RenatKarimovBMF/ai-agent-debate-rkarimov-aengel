Weighing the clash: my opponent rests on irreversibility, but death is not the only irreversible harm. A forced pregnancy is permanent, bodily, and sometimes fatal. The WHO finds that restricting access does not reduce abortion; it shifts it to unsafe procedures, killing tens of thousands of living women annually. His framework protects a contested interest by inflicting certain harm on an uncontested person. Mine protects both: it saves the women bans demonstrably kill while leaving the moral choice with the one party who must bear it in her body.
